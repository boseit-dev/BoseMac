# BoseMac

A quiet, minimal set of tools for your Mac.

- **BoseIsland**: a Dynamic Island for the MacBook notch. It shows music, timers, downloads, the volume and brightness level, and AI answers. Drop files on it to hold them.
- **BoseLens**: select anything on screen (text, a maths question, code, a chart, a photo) and ask AI to explain, solve, translate or identify it. Text recognition runs on your Mac.
- **Clipboard history**, **file shelf**, **launcher** (apps, files, maths, unit conversion and commands), **window snapping**, **screenshots and recording**, **system monitor**, **focus timer**, **notes**, **developer tools** and **automations**.
- Dark, Cream and Clear (glass) themes.

## Download

Get the latest **`BoseMac-…-universal.zip`** from **[Releases](https://github.com/boseit-dev/BoseMac/releases/latest)**. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 or later.

1. Unzip it and drag **BoseMac** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open it. Because BoseMac isn't from the App Store, macOS asks the first time. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
3. The setup guide explains each permission:
   - **Screen Recording** lets BoseLens read only the area you select, after you press the shortcut.
   - **Accessibility** lets it read highlighted text, snap windows and show its own volume/brightness indicator.

## Updates

BoseMac checks for updates once a day; you can also go to **Settings → Updates → Check Now**. Every update is digitally signed, and BoseMac refuses to install anything that doesn't carry the developer's signature.

## AI

AI works out of the box with a shared key, which has a daily limit per Mac. For your own limits, add your own free key in **Settings → AI Providers**. Groq, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and local models are supported.

## Privacy

- Your clipboard history, notes and AI history stay on your Mac, encrypted.
- BoseMac has no tracking, analytics or accounts.
- Nothing is sent to an AI service until you ask. BoseLens can show you exactly what will be sent, and blur parts of the image first.

## Default shortcuts

| | |
|---|---|
| BoseLens — capture | ⌘⇧Space |
| Ask about highlighted text | ⌃⌘L |
| Clipboard history | ⌃⌘V |
| Search | ⌥Space |
| Screenshot | ⌘⇧2 |
| Snap windows | ⌃⌥ + arrow keys |

You can change all of these in Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts.
